How it works

  1. Build the graph. Each subject is a vertex. Two subjects share an edge when any student sits both.
  2. Try k slots. Subjects are taken highest-degree first and given the lowest slot that no neighbor uses and that is not full.
  3. Backtrack. A subject with no legal slot undoes the previous choice and tries the next slot. Symmetric colorings are pruned.
  4. Keep the minimum. k grows from 1 until a full coloring exists, so the first success is the minimum. Over 30 subjects it starts at a clique lower bound and stops after 4 s with a greedy answer.
